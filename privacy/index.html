Privacy policy

Private by architecture, not just by policy.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

The short version

LocalConvert does not collect, store or transmit your files or personal data. Conversions, compression, editing and previews all run on your device. On the App Store, LocalConvert's privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Your files

  • Files you open are read from Photos, Files or the share sheet with your permission and processed locally.
  • Converted files are saved inside the app's own storage (visible in Files › On My iPhone › LocalConvert) and wherever you choose to save or share them.
  • Originals are never modified or deleted.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Photo library access

LocalConvert uses the system photo picker, which gives the app access only to the photos you select. If you choose "Save to Photos", the app requests add-only permission to save results.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not use your data to track you across apps or websites.

Network use

The app works fully offline. If you tap a link to this website or the App Store, that page is opened in your browser.

This website

This website is a static site. The browser image converter processes images inside your browser; they are never uploaded. We load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of the request. We do not set advertising cookies.

Children

LocalConvert is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Contact support.
