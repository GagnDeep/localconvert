What is a PDF file?

PDF preserves layout, fonts and images exactly, which is why it is the standard for contracts, forms, invoices and printing. LocalConvert can create PDFs from photos and documents, merge, split, reorder and compress them, or turn pages into images.

How to open PDF files

iPhone & iPad
Tap it in Files to preview with Quick Look. LocalConvert converts it to images, merges or compresses it.
Mac
Preview opens PDFs; Pages, Numbers and Keynote import Office files.
Windows
Opens with the built-in apps.
