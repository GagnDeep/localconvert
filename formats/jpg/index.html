What is a JPG file?

JPG uses lossy compression tuned for photographs, trading invisible detail for dramatically smaller files. Every phone, browser, email client and printer can open it, which makes it the safest choice when you need a photo to just work. It does not support transparency or animation.

What's the best format to convert JPG to?

For most people, PNG. Lossless images with full transparency — ideal for screenshots and graphics. LocalConvert can also convert JPG to HEIC, WebP, GIF and TIFF.

Is JPG lossless?

No. JPG uses lossy compression, discarding detail that is hard to notice to make files smaller.
