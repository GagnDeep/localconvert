What is a DOCX file?

DOCX is Word's XML-based format and the most common document type in offices and schools. LocalConvert previews DOCX files and turns them into PDFs or images right on your iPhone — no Word subscription required.

Mac
Preview opens PDFs; Pages, Numbers and Keynote import Office files.
