PNG file format (.png)

Lossless images with full transparency — ideal for screenshots and graphics.

What is a PNG file?

PNG compresses images without discarding any pixels, so text, UI and line art stay razor sharp. It supports a full alpha channel for transparent backgrounds. Photos saved as PNG are usually much larger than the same photo as JPG or HEIC.

How to open PNG files

iPhone & iPad
Opens in Photos and Files. Use LocalConvert to convert it or remove its metadata.
Mac
Preview and Photos open most images, including HEIC and RAW.
Windows
Opens with the built-in apps.
