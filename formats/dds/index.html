Frequently asked questions

What is a .dds file?

DDS files store textures in GPU-friendly compressed formats and are common in PC games and modding. LocalConvert decodes them to standard images.

How do I open a DDS file on iPhone?

Use LocalConvert to view it.

What's the best format to convert DDS to?

For most people, PNG. Lossless images with full transparency — ideal for screenshots and graphics. LocalConvert can also convert DDS to JPG, HEIC, WebP and GIF.

Is DDS lossless?

No. DDS uses lossy compression, discarding detail that is hard to notice to make files smaller.

Is it safe to open DDS files with LocalConvert?

Yes. LocalConvert processes files on your device and never uploads them, so your images stay private.
