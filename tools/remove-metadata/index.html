About remove location & metadata

Photos and videos quietly carry where and when they were taken. LocalConvert shows you what's inside and removes it in one tap — before you post.

How to use Remove Location & Metadata

  1. Open LocalConvert and tap Tools, then Remove Location & Metadata.
  2. Choose one or more files from Photos or Files.
  3. Review the location, camera and date details LocalConvert found.
  4. Tap Remove to create clean copies.
  5. Tap Clean, then save the result to Photos or Files, or share it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove location & metadata on iPhone?

1. Open LocalConvert and tap Tools, then Remove Location & Metadata. 2. Choose one or more files from Photos or Files. 3. Review the location, camera and date details LocalConvert found. 4. Tap Remove to create clean copies. 5. Tap Clean, then save the result to Photos or Files, or share it.

Which formats does Remove Location & Metadata support?

Remove Location & Metadata works with 48 formats, including JPG, MP4, PNG, HEIC, MOV, WebP, GIF and SVG.

Is Remove Location & Metadata free and private?

Yes. It's built into LocalConvert and runs entirely on your device — your files are never uploaded to a server, and there are no watermarks.

Does Remove Location & Metadata change my original file?

No. LocalConvert always creates a new file and leaves the original untouched in Photos or Files.
