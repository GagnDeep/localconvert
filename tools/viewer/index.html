About universal viewer

One viewer for everything: zoom into RAW photos, play video, read PDFs and Word documents, spin 3D models and place them in AR.

How to use Universal Viewer

  1. Open LocalConvert and tap Tools, then Universal Viewer.
  2. Choose a file from Photos or Files.
  3. Zoom, scrub, page through or spin it in 3D.
  4. Tap Open, then save the result to Photos or Files, or share it.

Why LocalConvert

Frequently asked questions

How do I universal viewer on iPhone?

1. Open LocalConvert and tap Tools, then Universal Viewer. 2. Choose a file from Photos or Files. 3. Zoom, scrub, page through or spin it in 3D. 4. Tap Open, then save the result to Photos or Files, or share it.

Which formats does Universal Viewer support?

Universal Viewer works with 101 formats, including JPG, MP4, PDF, MP3, PNG, HEIC, DOCX and MOV.

Is Universal Viewer free and private?

Yes. It's built into LocalConvert and runs entirely on your device — your files are never uploaded to a server, and there are no watermarks.

Does Universal Viewer change my original file?

No. LocalConvert always creates a new file and leaves the original untouched in Photos or Files.
