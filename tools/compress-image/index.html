    About compress images

    Batch-compress photos with a live before/after preview and size estimate. Optionally resize to a maximum dimension and strip location data in the same pass.

    How to use Compress Images

    1. Open LocalConvert and tap Tools, then Compress Images.
    2. Choose one or more files from Photos or Files.
    3. Choose a quality level; the estimated size updates as you go.
    4. Optionally set a maximum size and remove location data.
    5. Tap Compress, then save the result to Photos or Files, or share it.

    Why LocalConvert

    How do I compress images on iPhone?

    Which formats does Compress Images support?

    Compress Images works with 43 formats, including JPG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, GIF, SVG, AVIF and PSD.

    Is Compress Images free and private?

    Yes. It's built into LocalConvert and runs entirely on your device — your files are never uploaded to a server, and there are no watermarks.

    Does Compress Images change my original file?

    No. LocalConvert always creates a new file and leaves the original untouched in Photos or Files.
