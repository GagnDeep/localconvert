About compress pdf

Re-encodes the images inside a PDF at a lower resolution and quality while keeping text crisp and selectable where possible. Pick from Light, Balanced or Maximum compression.

How to use Compress PDF

  1. Open LocalConvert and tap Tools, then Compress PDF.
  2. Choose one or more files from Photos or Files.
  3. Choose Light, Balanced or Maximum compression.
  4. Tap Compress, then save the result to Photos or Files, or share it.
