    Frequently asked questions

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. This converter uses your browser's built-in image decoder and canvas to re-encode images on your own device. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works.

    Which formats can I convert?

    You can open JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF (first frame), BMP, ICO, SVG and AVIF in every modern browser, plus HEIC in Safari 17 or later. You can save as JPG, PNG or WebP (WebP output depends on your browser).

    Is there a file size limit?

    There's no upload limit because nothing is uploaded. Very large images may be slow on older devices since your browser does the work.

    Why use the app instead?

    The LocalConvert app supports 100+ formats including RAW, video, audio and PDF, converts in batches with Apple's hardware encoders, keeps metadata if you want, and saves straight to Photos.
