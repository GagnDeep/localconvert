Help & support

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Contact support — a real person replies, usually within a day.

Common questions

Where are my converted files saved?

In the Library tab, and in the Files app under On My iPhone › LocalConvert › Converted. You can also save each result to Photos or any folder.

A file says it can't be opened. What can I do?

Make sure it has finished downloading if it's in iCloud Drive or another cloud service. If it's a format LocalConvert doesn't support yet, check the formats list — and email us, we prioritise the most-requested formats.

Why can't I convert a song from Apple Music?

Streaming tracks are protected by DRM, which LocalConvert does not remove. Use audio files you own.

Can I convert MKV, AVI or WebM videos?

Not yet. LocalConvert uses Apple's native video frameworks, which support MOV, MP4, M4V and 3GP. Support for more containers is on our roadmap.

Why is my converted video larger than the original?

Converting to a less efficient codec (for example HEVC to H.264) or a higher quality preset can increase size. Use Compress Video with a target size to control it.

Does LocalConvert need internet access?

No. Everything works offline.

How do I open a file from another app in LocalConvert?

Use the share sheet and choose LocalConvert, or long-press a file in Files and choose Share › LocalConvert.
