About LocalConvert

A file converter that respects you and your files.

LocalConvert started with a simple frustration: converting a HEIC photo for an upload form shouldn't require sending it to a stranger's server, sitting through ads, and hitting a file-size limit.

Phones are now powerful enough to do this work themselves. Apple's frameworks can read and write a remarkable range of formats, and every recent iPhone has dedicated hardware for video encoding. We built LocalConvert to put all of that behind one calm, friendly interface.

What we believe

  • Your files are yours. Processing happens on your device. We couldn't see your files if we wanted to.
  • Simple beats clever. One decision per screen, with smart defaults that are right most of the time.
  • Honest numbers. Every format count on this site is generated from the same catalog the app uses.

By the numbers

  • 100+ formats recognised, 40 formats created
  • 672 conversion paths
  • 25 tools for compressing, trimming, merging and more

Have a format you need? Tell us. Want to learn more? Start with our blog or browse every supported format.
