Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every conversion, compression and edit runs on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's built-in frameworks. LocalConvert has no servers that receive your files, and it works in Airplane Mode.

Which formats does LocalConvert support?

LocalConvert opens 101 formats and writes 40, for 672 different conversions — photos (HEIC, JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF), camera RAW, video (MOV, MP4), audio (MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC), documents (PDF, Word, Pages, Excel), data, archives and 3D models. See every format.

Will converting reduce the quality of my photos or videos?

Only if you want it to. The default Balanced setting is visually lossless for most content. Choose Best to keep maximum quality, or Smallest when you need tiny files. Lossless formats like PNG, FLAC and TIFF never lose detail.

Can I convert lots of files at once?

Yes. Pick as many photos, videos or documents as you like and LocalConvert converts them in one batch, then lets you save them all to Photos or Files.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Because everything happens on-device, LocalConvert works anywhere — on a plane, on the subway, or with mobile data turned off.

What does it cost?

LocalConvert is free to download and try. There are no accounts, no watermarks and no file-size limits imposed by a server.
