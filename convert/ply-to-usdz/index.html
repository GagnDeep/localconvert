What is PLY?

PLY stores meshes and point clouds, often with per-vertex colour. It is common output from 3D scanning apps and photogrammetry.

What is USDZ?

USDZ packages a 3D scene and its textures into one file that iPhone, iPad, Mac and Vision Pro can display in AR Quick Look. Converting OBJ or STL to USDZ lets you place models in your room.
