What is CR3?

CR3 is Canon's camera RAW format. It keeps the untouched sensor data so you can recover highlights, shadows and white balance. It replaced CR2 on Canon's mirrorless EOS R and recent DSLR bodies. LocalConvert develops the RAW file on-device and exports a ready-to-share JPG, HEIC, PNG or TIFF.

What is PDF?

PDF preserves layout, fonts and images exactly, which is why it is the standard for contracts, forms, invoices and printing. LocalConvert can create PDFs from photos and documents, merge, split, reorder and compress them, or turn pages into images.
