What is M4V?

M4V is almost identical to MP4 and is used for videos from Apple's TV app and iTunes. Unprotected M4V files can be renamed or converted to MP4 instantly.

What is AIFF?

AIFF holds uncompressed PCM audio like WAV but supports richer metadata. It is common in Logic Pro, GarageBand and professional audio work on the Mac.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert M4V to AIFF on iPhone?

Will I lose quality converting M4V to AIFF?

No. AIFF is uncompressed, so the converted file keeps all the detail that was in your M4V. The file may be larger as a result.
