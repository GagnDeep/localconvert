What is NRW?

NRW is Nikon's camera RAW format. It keeps the untouched sensor data so you can recover highlights, shadows and white balance. It is produced by Nikon Coolpix compact cameras. LocalConvert develops the RAW file on-device and exports a ready-to-share JPG, HEIC, PNG or TIFF.

What is PNG?

PNG compresses images without discarding any pixels, so text, UI and line art stay razor sharp. It supports a full alpha channel for transparent backgrounds. Photos saved as PNG are usually much larger than the same photo as JPG or HEIC.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert NRW to PNG on iPhone?

Open LocalConvert, choose your NRW file from Photos or Files, select PNG and tap Convert. You can then save the PNG to Photos or Files, or share it directly.

Will I lose quality converting NRW to PNG?

No. PNG is lossless, so the converted file keeps all the detail that was in your NRW. The file may be larger as a result.

Can I convert multiple NRW files to PNG at once?

Yes. Select as many NRW files as you like and LocalConvert converts them all in one batch.

Is it safe to convert NRW to PNG with LocalConvert?

Yes. Unlike online converters, LocalConvert never uploads your files. The conversion runs on your iPhone's processor, so private photos, documents and recordings stay on your device. Read why that matters.

Does converting NRW to PNG keep the date and location?

By default LocalConvert keeps metadata such as capture date and camera details. Turn on Remove location & metadata in Options to strip GPS coordinates before sharing.

Can I convert NRW to PNG on Windows or Mac?
