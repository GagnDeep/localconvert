    What is HEIC?

    HEIC stores images with HEVC (H.265) compression inside a HEIF container. iPhones have used it by default since iOS 11 because it keeps more detail at roughly half the size of JPG and supports HDR and depth data. Many Windows PCs, older Android phones and web upload forms still can't open it, which is why HEIC to JPG is one of the most common conversions.

    What is JPG?

    JPG uses lossy compression tuned for photographs, trading invisible detail for dramatically smaller files. Every phone, browser, email client and printer can open it, which makes it the safest choice when you need a photo to just work. It does not support transparency or animation.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert HEIC to JPG on iPhone?

    Open LocalConvert, choose your HEIC file from Photos or Files, select JPG and tap Convert. You can then save the JPG to Photos or Files, or share it directly.

    Will I lose quality converting HEIC to JPG?

    Re-encoding a lossy file always discards a little information, but at LocalConvert's default Balanced setting the difference is invisible for everyday use. Choose Best in Options for maximum quality.

    Can I convert multiple HEIC files to JPG at once?

    Yes. Select as many HEIC files as you like and LocalConvert converts them all in one batch.

    Is it safe to convert HEIC to JPG with LocalConvert?

    Yes. Unlike online converters, LocalConvert never uploads your files. The conversion runs on your iPhone's processor, so private photos, documents and recordings stay on your device. Read why that matters.

    Does converting HEIC to JPG keep the date and location?

    By default LocalConvert keeps metadata such as capture date and camera details. Turn on Remove location & metadata in Options to strip GPS coordinates before sharing.

    Does JPG keep the transparent background?

    JPG doesn't support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with a solid colour — white by default. Convert to PNG or WebP instead if you need to keep transparency.
