What is PDF?

PDF preserves layout, fonts and images exactly, which is why it is the standard for contracts, forms, invoices and printing. LocalConvert can create PDFs from photos and documents, merge, split, reorder and compress them, or turn pages into images.

What is TXT?

Plain text files contain only characters — no fonts or layout — so they are tiny, future-proof and readable by every device. LocalConvert extracts text from PDFs and rich documents into TXT.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert PDF to TXT on iPhone?

Open LocalConvert, choose your PDF file from Photos or Files, select TXT and tap Convert. You can then save the TXT to Photos or Files, or share it directly.

Can I convert multiple PDF files to TXT at once?

Yes. Select as many PDF files as you like and LocalConvert converts them all in one batch.

Is it safe to convert PDF to TXT with LocalConvert?

Yes. Unlike online converters, LocalConvert never uploads your files. The conversion runs on your iPhone's processor, so private photos, documents and recordings stay on your device. Read why that matters.

Can I convert PDF to TXT on Windows or Mac?

LocalConvert is built for iPhone and iPad and also runs on Apple silicon Macs. On Windows you'll need a desktop converter, or convert on your iPhone and AirDrop or email the result.
